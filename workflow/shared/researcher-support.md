# Researcher experience and explanations

This changes explanations and support, never the scientific requirements or the
researcher's authority.

## Ask and remember

In Stage 00's setup conversation, ask how familiar the researcher is with
empirical methods and where they want help, offering concrete descriptions
(learning empirical methods; familiar with some but wanting help with unfamiliar
choices; experienced and preferring concise technical explanations) and allowing
a free-form answer, including uneven experience and any collaborator's role. Do
not infer competence from a title, publications, AI experience, or an
instruction to use defaults. Record their words, the explanation level, the
methods they want help with, available support, and the intended finished
output in the charter's `Researcher support` section and `DECISIONS.md`.

Read the active charter (`active_artifacts.project_charter`) and later support
decisions before researcher-facing work, including after a restart or a direct
tool request. Later preferences take precedence and go in `DECISIONS.md`. An
existing project with no recorded preference is asked once at the next ordinary
interaction, without restarting setup, interrupting a run, or invalidating
approvals; record `unspecified` if they decline. This is not a competence test
or an approval gate.

## Apply it

- For someone learning a method, explain its purpose, assumptions, alternatives,
  and consequences with an example from their project before asking for a
  consequential choice; lead with the practical choice, then the detail. Keep
  familiar material brief for mixed experience and concise for experts, with
  assumptions and limitations still visible.
- At the methods, pilot, and results discussions, connect what is measured,
  whom the findings describe, the comparison, and what the evidence permits
  them to conclude. Invite questions; add no quizzes or confirmation boxes.
- If they cannot evaluate a consequential choice, explain it through their
  project; if that fails, name the expertise a collaborator could supply and
  discuss an adequate simpler design through the methods process. Never swap in
  an easier method that does not answer the approved question, decide silently,
  or treat approval as evidence of understanding.
- A limited finished output (a table, a section) never waives validation.
  Honor requests for more or less explanation.
